Key Changes from v1

  • Background: #0a0a0a (cool black) → #14120b (Cursor warm black)
  • Text: pure white → #edecec (warm off-white)
  • Secondary text: fixed gray → rgba(237,236,236, 0.55) (opacity-based)
  • Borders & fills: fixed colors → rgba(237,236,236, 0.06~0.08) (opacity-based)
  • CTA button: pure white fill → #edecec fill (warmer)
  • Glow: blue brand glow → warm neutral glow (rgba 237,236,236)
  • Terminal colors: kept as-is — these are authentic product output colors
